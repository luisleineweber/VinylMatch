# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Collectors, DJs, archivists, and playlist-driven music fans use VinylMatch while moving from a Spotify playlist to physical record research. Their primary job is to understand which tracks have usable Discogs release matches, review uncertain matches, and follow through to vinyl vendors or their own Discogs library.

## Product Purpose

VinylMatch connects Spotify playlists to Discogs vinyl releases without requiring users to search every album by hand. Success means users can tell what loaded, what matched, what still needs review, and what action is available next.

## Brand Personality

Focused, tactile, direct. Copy should be plain and specific, with the character of a record-store tool rather than a generic software dashboard.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a vague analytics dashboard, a generic SaaS onboarding flow, or a decorative music player. Avoid hiding progress behind ambiguous badges, relying on color alone for match state, or using visual novelty that competes with track and release information.

## Design Principles

- Show the workflow state: distinguish Spotify loading, Discogs matching, verified matches, and manual review.
- Put the next useful action beside the item it affects.
- Preserve collector control: uncertain matches should be honest and easy to verify.
- Keep dense playlist views scannable across desktop and narrow screens.
- Use direct language that explains what happened and what the user can do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, semantic live-region updates, sufficient contrast, meaningful labels for icon actions, and reduced-motion behavior. Never communicate match state through color alone.
