About VinylMatch

Connect digital playlists with physical releases

What is VinylMatch?

VinylMatch bridges your digital playlists and the physical record world. Add a Spotify playlist to find releases on Discogs. Review each match, choose a pressing, and compare record stores.

Key features

  • Spotify integration — Sign in to browse your own playlists.
  • Smart matching — Compare track and album details with Discogs releases.
  • Vendor links — One-click search across HHV, JPC, Amazon, and custom vendors.
  • Recent playlists — Pick up right where you left off with instant access to your last sessions.
  • Curation tools — Fine-tune results manually for obscure or mismatched releases.
